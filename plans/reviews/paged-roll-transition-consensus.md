A truncated later part can therefore crash when the newly enabled paged-to-Roll transition builds its first window. Reject inconsistent part lengths or validate each part before indexing.

- **P2 — View round-trips do not preserve transport intent.** [score_runtime.cpp:1122](/C:/Users/cmaughan/AppData/Local/Temp/draxul-review-codex-vks69pbf/workspace/product/draxul-scoreview/src/score_runtime.cpp:1122) re-arms only `Roll`.
